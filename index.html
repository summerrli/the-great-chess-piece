A supportive, high-energy way to learn the game

The Great Chess Piece is a cost-free, hands-on chess program designed to inspire and challenge young minds. Taught by two nationally ranked women's chess players with extensive tutoring experience, this program provides a supportive, high-energy environment for students to master the basics, build strategic thinking, and have an engaging introduction to the game.

A supportive, high-energy way to learn the game

The Great Chess Piece is a cost-free, hands-on chess program designed to inspire and challenge young minds. Taught by two nationally ranked women's chess players with extensive tutoring experience, this program provides a supportive, high-energy environment for students to master the basics, build strategic thinking, and have an engaging introduction to the game.

Meet your coaches

Two nationally ranked women's chess players bring real tournament experience and years of tutoring to every session.

Madison Wu

Co-Founder & Head Coach, Beginners · Nationally Ranked Player

Madison has played competitive chess for 11 years and is a nationally ranked female player and co-captain of the Riverdale Country School chess team. She co-founded The Great Chess Piece to give more girls a seat at the board and the confidence to stay there. Off the board, she's a third-degree black belt in taekwondo who competes internationally, and she spends her spare hours doing astrophysics research.

Summer Li

Co-Founder & Head Coach, Intermediate/Advanced · Nationally Ranked Player

Summer has played chess for 7 years and has been nationally ranked for the past 3. She co-founded The Great Chess Piece after noticing the gender imbalance in competitive chess, and she wants more girls to feel they belong at the board. A co-leader of her school's math team, she loves machine learning and has spent three years doing research in astrophysics, neuroscience, bioinformatics, and now computational chess. Her art has won national awards and been exhibited at the Met. Off the board, you'll find her at a concert.
